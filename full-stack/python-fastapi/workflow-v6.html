⚡ Why POST?
We use POST because the transfer creates a new resource (a transaction) and changes data on the server. It’s not a read; it’s an action with side effects.
🛡️ Why authentication?
We must verify it’s really Anita, check her permissions, and ensure she can access the account. This protects customer data and prevents fraud.
✅ What validation checks?
  • Amount is positive and within limits
  • Sender has sufficient balance
  • Both accounts exist and are in the same bank
  • Account is active and not restricted
  • Fraud and risk checks (velocity, block lists, etc.)
🗂️ How the DB rows change?
  • A new transaction row is created (PROCESSING → SUCCESS)
  • Sender’s account balance is debited (₹2,000 → ₹1,500)
  • Receiver’s account balance is credited (₹700 → ₹1,200)
  • Two ledger entries are written (one DEBIT, one CREDIT)

@app.post("/api/v1/transfer")
async def transfer(request: TransferRequest,
        user: User = Depends(get_current_user)):
    # 1. Validate input
    validate_transfer_request(request)
    # 2. Check accounts and balance
    assert same_bank(request.from_account, request.to_account)
    # 3. Execute transfer (within a transaction)
    async with db.transaction():
        await debit(request.from_account, request.amount)
        await credit(request.to_account, request.amount)
        await create_transaction_record(request, user.id)
        await create_ledger_entries(request)
    return {"status": "success", "txn_id": txn_id}

💡 Why create this request?
It tells Nova Bank to move ₹500 from Anita to Rahul. The request includes the beneficiary’s account and IFSC so the money can reach the correct bank through the payment network.
🏦 Why separate banks?
Each bank manages its own customers’ accounts and data. A trusted payment network connects banks and securely routes payment messages between them.
✔ What is acknowledged?
The receiving bank confirms successful credit of funds to Rahul’s account. This acknowledgment is sent back through the network to Nova Bank, completing the transfer.
⚠ What if receiving bank fails?
Nova Bank will not lose the money. The transfer is retried or reversed based on network rules. The debit may be held in a pending state and automatically reconciled.

📄 Why POST /checkout?
We use POST because we're creating something (an order) and it changes data on the server (not just reading).
🛡️ Why validate quantity?
We must check the product exists and the requested quantity is available to prevent overselling and errors.

We authorize the payment with a trusted provider (e.g., Stripe) to securely charge the customer's payment method.
🗄️ Why reserve stock first?
We reserve the item before charging to avoid taking payment for an item we can't fulfill.
